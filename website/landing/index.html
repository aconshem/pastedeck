Not just a clipboard manager

PasteBoard keeps everything you retype in a day within reach: replies, client details, files, forms and reminders.

Command bar

Press Ctrl+Shift+Space on any page. Search snippets, files, reminders, desks and sessions, then press Enter to use the result.

Desks

One workspace per kind of work: recruitment, email, visas. PasteBoard switches desks by website, and you can pin one by hand.

Snippets

Insert a saved reply straight into the field you are typing in, or type a short code like ;sal. If no field is focused, it copies instead.

Candidate sessions

Keep one person's passport, phone, ID and salary together across WhatsApp Web, Gmail and forms. The session deletes itself when its timer ends.

Capture blueprints

Click a field, name it, repeat. On the next client page of the same website, one click copies every detail into the session.

Paste blueprints

Map a form once. Next time, preview the values and fill the whole form with one click.

File shelf

Keep your logo, location and requirements PDF ready. Passport and full-body photos expire with the session.

Clipboard history

Search text, links and images you copied. Pin what you need. Password fields are never recorded.

Tasks and reminders

Stop messaging yourself on WhatsApp. Type “Call medical tomorrow 9am” and Chrome reminds you, with snooze and repeats.

How does pricing work?

There are two plans, and you only ever pay once.

Free ($0) is for trying PasteBoard on one computer: 1 seat (you), 1 desk, 1 snippet, and 1 candidate session at a time. Capture & paste blueprints and the file shelf are switched off. Clipboard history, the command bar and tasks work.

Premium ($25, one time) removes every limit — unlimited snippets, desks and sessions, blueprints, autofill and the file shelf — and includes 5 seats.

Extra seats cost $2.50 each, one time. Example: Premium ($25) + 5 extra seats (5 × $2.50 = $12.50) = 10 seats for $37.50 in total. Seats you buy are yours for good; if someone leaves, you can give their seat to somebody else.

What is a seat? One person. That person can use PasteBoard on as many of their own computers as they like (but signed in on one at a time). The account owner uses one seat.

No subscriptions. No monthly fees, no renewals, no automatic charges. Prices are in USD.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Each account can start one 14-day Premium trial from the dashboard after verifying its email. No card is asked for and nothing is charged afterwards — when the trial ends the account returns to Free. Your local data is never deleted.

Why can I only be signed in on one device?

An account is for one person at a time. If you sign in on a second device, the first one is signed out automatically (and the other way round). Your data is not affected; it lives on each computer. To move data between computers use Export and Import in the extension.

I forgot my password. What now?

Choose “Forgot your password?” on the login page and we’ll email you a one-time link, valid for 30 minutes. If you registered a passkey you can also sign in with it and then set a new password in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer, in Chrome’s local extension storage. There is no cloud sync: clipboard history, snippets, desks, tasks, sessions, blueprints and files are never uploaded. Our servers only hold your account, plan, team and billing records — plus simple usage totals if you choose to share them.

How do I move to another computer?

Use Export in the extension’s settings to save a JSON backup and Import it on the other computer.

Is this just a clipboard manager?

No. Clipboard history is one feature. PasteBoard also keeps snippets, candidate sessions, blueprints for capturing and filling form fields, tasks and a command bar.

What is a blueprint?

A blueprint is a memory of where things are on a website. You click a field once and name it, and PasteBoard stores the location so you can capture or paste into it later. Blueprints do not use AI. They are part of Premium.

What happens when a session expires?

You choose 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or end of day. When time is up, the session and its files are deleted from your computer.

How do refunds work?

Full refund within 14 days of purchase. See the refund policy.

Is this just a clipboard manager?

Does it work on WhatsApp Web and Gmail?

Yes. Snippets insert into the message box you are typing in. If a site blocks direct insertion, PasteBoard copies the text so you can paste it yourself.

What is a blueprint?

A blueprint is a memory of where things are on a website. You click a field once and name it. PasteBoard stores the location, so on the next page of that site it can read the same fields, or fill the same form.

Do blueprints use AI?

No. They use the page structure you pointed at. That keeps them fast, predictable and private. If a site changes its layout, teach it again.

What happens when a session expires?

You choose 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or end of day. When time is up, the session and its files are deleted.

Can my team share snippets and files?

Owners and admins can set company details and share snippets, desks, images and blueprints with staff. Team sync arrives with the cloud release.

Two steps to get going

1. Install the extension. 2. Sign up (or log in, if you already have an account) to activate it. The Chrome Web Store listing is on its way — until then, load the extension yourself in a minute.

Create your account Log in
  1. Download the PasteBoard folder and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  4. Press Ctrl+Shift+Space on any page.
  5. Sign up or log in (buttons on the left) to activate your account.
