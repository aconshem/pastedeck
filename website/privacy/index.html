Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed against your actual data flows and the privacy laws that apply to you (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) before relying on it — particularly if you handle candidates' sensitive personal data as part of recruiting work.

This Privacy Policy explains what information PasteBoard collects, how it's used, and your choices. It covers the PasteBoard browser extension and website together (the "Service").

1. The short version

  • Your working data — desks, snippets, candidate sessions, blueprints, files, clipboard history, tasks, and leads — is stored locally in your browser (via the extension's local storage), not on our servers, unless a specific feature says otherwise.
  • Account-level data — your email, username, name, a hashed password, your plan, trial status, and your team's device/login roster — is stored on our servers (currently hosted on Netlify) so that accounts, logins, and team approval can work across devices.
  • If you turn on usage analytics syncing, we also store day-by-day counts (like "12 snippets used") per device, so the account owner can see team-wide usage — never the content of what you typed, pasted, or captured.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

2. Information we collect

a) Account information (stored on our servers)

  • Username, full name, email address;
  • A salted, hashed password (we never store or can see your actual password);
  • Your plan, trial dates, and billing status (we don't store full card numbers — see Section 6);
  • Your account's device/user roster: names, emails, roles, and approval status of anyone with access to the account;
  • If you reach out to us for support, the contents of that correspondence.

b) Local extension/browser data (stays on your device)

  • Desks, snippets, candidate sessions, blueprint field locations, files you add to the file shelf, clipboard history, tasks, and leads;
  • This data lives in your browser's local extension storage. It is not transmitted to our servers by the current version of the Service. Exporting a backup (Settings → Export) creates a file on your own device that only you control.
  • Candidate sessions are designed to expire automatically (15 minutes to end of day, your choice) and are deleted from your device when they do.

c) Usage analytics (opt-in sync)

  • If analytics syncing is enabled, your device periodically sends aggregate daily counts (snippets used, forms autofilled, fields captured, estimated time saved) to our servers, tagged to your device, so the account owner (or someone they've granted access) can see team-wide usage trends.
  • This never includes the text of your snippets, session contents, or anything you typed or captured — only counts.

d) Technical information

  • Basic device/browser information (e.g. "Chrome on macOS") shown in your Team & Devices page, so you can recognize your own devices;
  • Standard web server logs (IP address, timestamps, pages requested) kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, and to let you and anyone you approve log in;
  • To operate the team/device approval workflow and enforce plan limits;
  • To show you usage analytics, if enabled;
  • To communicate with you about your account, trial, or support requests;
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Candidate and third-party data

If you use PasteBoard for recruiting-style workflows, you may enter data about other people (candidates, clients) into sessions, blueprints, or leads. That data stays in your local browser storage as described above. You, not PasteBoard, are the data controller for that information under most privacy laws, and you're responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and use it, and for honoring any rights those individuals have over their own data.

5. Where data is stored and who can see it

Account-level data is currently stored using Netlify's infrastructure (Netlify Blobs) as our backend database. Only you (as account owner), anyone you've approved on your account (limited to their own login and, if granted, analytics), and PasteBoard's own founder/operator (for platform administration, via a separately access-controlled admin panel) can access account data. We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Payments

We don't store full card numbers. Once a payment processor is connected, card details will be handled directly by that processor (acting as merchant of record), and we'll only retain what's needed to show you your billing status (e.g. plan, trial end date, and a payment record limited to amount, plan, and date).

7. Cookies and local storage

The website uses browser local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. The extension uses Chrome's local extension storage for all of its working data, described above.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep account-level data for as long as your account is active. You can delete local extension data at any time (Settings → Reset). To delete your account entirely, contact us at the email below; we'll remove your account record and associated devices/roster within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain limited records (e.g. payment history) for legal or accounting reasons.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business/professional use and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise a privacy right? Contact us at privacy@pasteboard.com.
