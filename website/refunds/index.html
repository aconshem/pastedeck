Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a template, not legal advice. Confirm it matches the policy you actually configure with your payment processor (e.g. Paddle, as merchant of record, applies its own buyer terms alongside this policy) before launch.

1. Free trial

Premium and Premium Pro both start with a 14-day free trial. You are not charged during the trial, so there's nothing to refund during that period — you can simply stop using the Service, or downgrade, before the trial ends.

2. One-time plan purchases (Premium, Premium Pro)

If you're charged for a one-time Premium or Premium Pro purchase after your trial ends, you may request a full refund within 14 days of that charge, no questions asked. After 14 days, purchases are non-refundable, except where required by law.

3. Additional devices/users (per-seat add-on)

Additional device/user seats beyond a plan's included amount are billed per seat. If you remove a seat, it stops being billed going forward; we don't provide partial refunds for the current billing period already paid for that seat.

4. Cloud sync subscription (Premium Pro, when available)

Cloud sync is billed monthly. You can cancel anytime, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — we don't provide partial-month refunds, but you won't be charged again after cancelling.

5. How to request a refund

Email billing@pasteboard.com with the email address on your account and the approximate date of the charge. We aim to respond within 2 business days and, where a refund is approved, to process it within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

6. Payment processor terms

Once a payment processor is connected, it may act as the merchant of record for your purchase and apply its own buyer protection terms in addition to this policy. Where the two differ, whichever is more favorable to you applies, except where that would conflict with applicable law.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Updates apply to charges made after the change; we'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
