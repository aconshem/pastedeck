Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This is a template, not legal advice. Confirm it matches the policy you actually configure with your payment processor (e.g. Paddle, as merchant of record, applies its own buyer terms alongside this policy) before launch.

1. Free trial

Each eligible account can start one 14-day Premium trial without a card. You are never charged automatically, so there is nothing to refund; when it ends the account simply returns to Free and your local data stays untouched.

2. Premium ($25, one-time) and extra seats ($2.50 each, one-time)

You may ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. After 14 days purchases are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. When a refund is completed the entitlement it granted is withdrawn: a refunded Premium returns the account to Free, and refunded seats are removed (if that leaves more members than seats, the most recently added members lose access). Your local data is never deleted.

3. No subscriptions

PasteBoard has no subscriptions, recurring billing or automatic charges, so there is nothing to cancel.

4. How to request a refund

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE with the email address on your account and the approximate date of the charge. We aim to respond within 2 business days and, where a refund is approved, to process it within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

5. Payment processor terms

Payments are processed by Creem, which acts as merchant of record and applies its own buyer-protection terms in addition to this policy. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Updates apply to charges made after the change; we'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
